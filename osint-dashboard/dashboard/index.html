Each row is one side's use of one tactic, grouped by type. The color shows how widely it was being used, in a fixed order: emerging, spreading, widespread, fading, with resurgent for a comeback. Each color lasts until the next report, and a faded end means nothing newer has been reported. Reading this data

  • Loss rates are Ukrainian MoD claims of Russian losses. They are unverified, biased upward, and cover one side only; read them as a trend, not a count.
  • Oryx and WarSpotting count only visually confirmed losses, so they undercount. The Oryx figures here come from a mirror last updated in February 2023.
  • Observations are paraphrased from ISW, RUSI and news coverage. Many ISW items were read through news reports of its assessments and are marked as secondary.
  • ISW and RUSI write from a Western perspective with significant Ukrainian access; Russian-side tactics are often described through Ukrainian reporting.
  • Territory comes from DeepStateMap, a Ukrainian volunteer project. Its map is close to ISW's but not identical, and some day-to-day changes are redrawing rather than fighting.
  • Trend fits and shift points are statistics on claimed numbers. A shift can reflect a change in what the ministry counts as much as a change on the battlefield, and the adaptation-speed figures rest on a few dozen observations.
  • Unit names are recorded only where a source gives them, and attributions sometimes conflict.
