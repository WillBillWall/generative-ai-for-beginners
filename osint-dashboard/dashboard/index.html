Reading this data

  • Loss rates are Ukrainian MoD claims of Russian losses. They are unverified, biased upward, and cover one side only; read them as a trend, not a count.
  • Oryx and WarSpotting count only visually confirmed losses, so they undercount. The Oryx figures here come from a mirror last updated in February 2023.
  • Observations are paraphrased from ISW, RUSI and news coverage. Many ISW items were read through news reports of its assessments and are marked as secondary.
  • ISW and RUSI write from a Western perspective with significant Ukrainian access; Russian-side tactics are often described through Ukrainian reporting.
  • Unit names are recorded only where a source gives them, and attributions sometimes conflict.
